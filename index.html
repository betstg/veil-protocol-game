The world

Tokyo, 2015. The city looks ordinary. It is not. The dead never fully left... they press against a thinning membrane called the veil, most of all the ones lost without a grave. A few of the living can feel them, read them, and sometimes cross over, and a quiet government bureau exists to keep everyone else from ever noticing.

Beneath the registered world of that bureau sit the old Tower families, the unregistered Grid, the cults that feed on the dead, and thirteen people who each hold one human emotion open like a door. Step into any of it, and the city turns out to be far larger, and far older, than you were told.

A phone full of people

Text the contacts your brother left behind. Real conversations that warm, cool, or curdle with how you treat them.

⌖
A city that moves on its own

People keep promises, gossip, follow leads, and live their lives whether or not you are watching.

⚔
Fight, or talk it down

Every encounter is a choice: force, or words. Free the person inside a possession instead of destroying them.

✖
Choices that stick

People can be lost for good. The world remembers what happened, and so do the ones who survive it.

♥
Bonds you earn

Friendship and romance are earned, never assumed. Adult routes are opt-in and 18+.

⛬
Five roads, many endings

The Directorate, the Towers, the Grid, the cult, the centre. Each can make you a hero, a tyrant, or a tragedy.

☿
A power that costs you

Reaching past your rank thins the veil, frays your mind, and draws the attention of something vast.
