# Veil Protocol: The Other Side — Quest & Endings Design (GM Book)

*Tokyo, August 2015. Obon. The membrane between living and dead is at its thinnest of the year, and in Kita-ku it is thinner still. This document is the playable spine: how the campaign opens, the five roads that open from it, the Axis at the centre, the two demons pulling at the edges, and the endings each road can earn — noble, dark, or tragic.*

> **Canon source.** This follows `content/registry/canon.json` (the trigger, the five routes, the moral spectrum, the Axis, the two-demon structure), `lore.json` (the campaign arc, the clock, the domain registry), the 70 character sheets, and the canonical rank scale: **0 Null · E Sensitive · D Postulate · C Adept · B Ritualist · A Arbiter · S Vanguard · SS Guardian · SSS Singularity · X Axis** (X must never surface to the player).

---

## 0. How the campaign runs

**Entry, not assignment.** Rei Uedera (19, Arbiter-A actual / presents Postulate-D under suppression, born with **two** psychic paths — the forbidden multi-path) is not given a route. His brother **Gin** goes silent on a Wednesday night. Searching for Gin is the only thing that pulls him across the threshold into the hidden world. Once inside, all five roads stand open at once as sandboxes.

**Follow your heart.** The player drifts toward the roads that match what they care about; the Warden grows whichever they lean into. The Gin thread runs *through* every road — never abandoned, but it stops being the only thing.

**One ending fires first.** Each road has a central variable. Whichever resolves earliest fires the ending; a late road can be foreclosed by an earlier one closing. There is no "win screen" — every road can reach its aim *and* cost Rei his decency, or fail him outright.

**The hidden clock (8 weeks, variable).** Gin was identified as a target 18 months ago; the possession completed the night he went quiet. Yushiro now rides him. The membrane around Kita thins on a schedule the player should *feel* before they understand it — Rei's own unmanaged Arbiter aperture is part of what is feeding it. The clock is the pressure under every road.

## 1. The Spine — Rei's three questions

These thread through every road. They are the through-line the Warden returns to.

### ① Find Gin
- **Hook.** Gin is silent since Wednesday; the CVD has *not* flagged him missing — someone wants it quiet. The flat is wrong: keys that don't fit, a cold spot, a door that locks itself (Iwata the handyman confirms it, unsuperstitious).
- **Stages.** Activate the Archive (four Grid nodes, locked behind contacts Rei has never met) → trace Gin's last week (the briefing he skipped, the case closed too fast, the address in no file) → learn the *shape* in Gin before naming it (a top-grade of Duty doesn't look like possession from outside) → reach a lucid window.
- **The texts.** Texting Gin now reaches the compromised Gin: sparse, a beat too calm, never says where he is, tries to make Rei *stop looking* ("let it go, go home, this is not for you") as the love bleeds through. Dread, not comfort.
- **Payoff.** Every road begins here. What Rei *becomes* on the way decides which ending the reason earns.

### ② What am I?
- **Hook.** Rei reads anomalous on the instruments; two paths awake from birth is not a defect the bureaus fear *for his sake* — it is exactly the kind of crosser the **Axis** collects.
- **Stages.** Notice the suppression is a cage *and* the only thing keeping him free to look → meet others who read wrong too (Tendo, the Pair, Choi Hana) → learn the Axis was once human and multi-path (Rei alone can suspect this) → decide whether to grow his power (each method that reaches Gin raises his real rank, and rank is a beacon).
- **Payoff.** This question is the secret engine of the Thirteen/Axis road and the cosmic danger of the endgame.

### ③ The Duty seat (XIII)
- **Hook.** Gin found the vacant Thirteenth Seat — **Duty**, empty since 2009, the campaign keystone, the open wound thinning Kita's membrane. Yushiro intends to *close it* by becoming its fixed occupant.
- **Stages.** Learn the Domain Registry (the twelve held seats + the vacant XIII) → understand why a vacant register is an open wound → discover Yushiro is recruiting through Gin's reputation, one reasonable favour at a time → face the choice: a fixed Yushiro as bulwark against the cult-demon, or a worse throne.
- **Payoff.** Feeds the endings directly. The seat resolving — well, corrupted, or too late — is the spine's central variable.

## 2. The Five Roads

*Each can yield a hero, a tyrant, or a tragedy. The "ending trigger" is the variable that, when it resolves, fires this road's ending.*

### Road A — The Thirteen Seats / Axis
**Aim.** Help structure the Veil alongside the balance-god and the Thirteen; take up the vacant Duty seat and hold the membrane.

**How it opens.** Question ②/③ pull here. The player learns the Domain Registry and that Rei's multi-path nature is *Axis-shaped*. Beyond-the-veil travel becomes possible: Rei can reach the Axis, who answers and then **shoves him away** with traces of abilities Rei doesn't yet possess.

**Key NPCs.** Director Adachi (first living authority to confirm the Thirteen), Yamamoto (the 22-year archive, the demon's true register), the domain holders (esp. **Watanabe Jun**, Despair VI, *weakening* — urgent), Yushiro (the rival occupant).

**Beats.** Map the twelve holders and the vacant XIII → reach Level 5 in all six paths (the Axis stops shoving only then) → stand at the seat → decide *how* to hold it.

**Ending trigger — the seat resolves.**
- **Noble.** Rei takes the seat well, steadies Kita, eases the Axis's burden, Gin is freed.
- **Dark.** Rei takes the seat *corrupted* — a holder turned toward the demon-case of his register, holding power instead of balance.
- **Tragedy.** The seat resolves too late or wrong; Gin dies, or Rei dissolves reaching too far into the centre.

> **The Axis turn (cosmic danger, never quite touched).** If Rei (or any strong multi-path crosser) maxes all six paths, the Axis *remembers it was human*, stops being mere balance, and begins absorbing the strong multi-path humans who make the passage — beginning to *care* again, which is the catastrophe. The campaign edges toward this and must never fully arrive. The **X / Axis rank is never named to the player.**

### Road B — The Directorate (CVD)
**Aim.** Organize the mediums and arrest the dangerous ones, from inside the official body.

**How it opens.** Get registered (presents Postulate-D), earn first clearance, work cases. Okada has been watching for Rei for four years on Gin's instruction; Adachi recognizes what Rei is.

**Key NPCs.** Okada (the four-year watch), Adachi (the ceiling), Moriya (Internal Affairs — shield or inquiry), Shimizu (suppression / the Orimoto containment that makes the exorcism viable), Noa (the unfiled truth of Gin's last months), Hagiwara Mizuki (Public Confidence — the cover-up that can erase or protect Rei), the Auditors (the bi-monthly assessment loop).

**Beats.** Registration → clearance → the assessment-anomaly thread (Okada → the Satō case) → Internal Affairs survives or buries the Gin discrepancy.

**Ending trigger — Rei's standing resolves (rises, is spent, or is scapegoated).**
- **Noble.** Reform from within; protect the vulnerable; bring real threats to justice without becoming a cog.
- **Dark.** Rise by becoming the institution's instrument — filing people away, sealing inconvenient truths (including Gin's).
- **Tragedy.** The system grinds him down or scapegoats him; the bad ones he chased were protected from above all along.

### Road C — The Tower Families
**Aim.** Help one of the five families command the CVD and train mediums under that family's flag.

**How it opens.** Through **Itsuki** to the Kuroda (the teleport lineage); Sei's grievance gates the family archive; Sena (Fujiwara-Kage Auditor) assesses Rei as a risk category.

**Key NPCs.** Itsuki (the prodigal who fled the name — and can be *bled for it*), Kuroda Sei (three generations of district intelligence, Silent Transit extraction), Sena (the Tower's verdict on Rei), the Tempering question, the Tenth Door.

**Beats.** Earn a family's interest → the Tempering question (what the family asks Rei to become) → the Tenth Door (the family's deepest leverage).

**Ending trigger — the patronage resolves.**
- **Noble.** Patronage that genuinely trains and shelters mediums; old money spent well; reach without a leash.
- **Dark.** Rei becomes a family's enforcer; mediums become assets under a flag; the CVD a tool of dynastic power.
- **Tragedy.** He is spent like a debt, bled for a name (as Itsuki can be), discarded when no longer useful.

### Road D — Find Your Brother / Stop the Cult *(the spine made a road)*
**Aim.** Save Gin and stop the cult-demon whose ascension to **Inverted God** would start a war.

**How it opens.** The Gin thread deepens into the cult: the **Far-Shore Circle / Higan-kai lineage** run by **Kishin** (in Fukai Rinka), feeding to become the dark mirror of the Axis. Yushiro is hunting it; Rei is caught between them.

**Key NPCs.** Gin/Yushiro (Duty, wants to discharge an interrupted duty — willing to drop the veil and merge living and dead — *not* power, not the Axis), Kishin/Fukai Rinka (the Inverted-God aspirant), Kurosawa (reads Gin's body — the double-layer signature, the demon's class), the spiritual practitioners (Egami, Pak, Anselm, Lin Wei, Baba — real exorcism outside CVD doctrine), Tanaka (the one operational fact, behind Kurosawa).

**Beats.** Diagnose what's in Gin (Kurosawa) → map Kishin's cult and feeding → the rite that fixes a crosser into a vacant register, and what it costs the host → choose between the two demons.

**Ending trigger — the possession & the cult resolve.**
- **Noble.** Gin is freed (damaged but present), the Inverted God is denied its ascent, the war is averted.
- **Dark.** Rei stops the cult by means that make him *its successor*, or lets Yushiro win to save Gin, dropping the veil.
- **Tragedy.** Gin dies; or the cult wins and the war between the 13 demons and the 13 mediums begins.

### Road E — The Grid (Clandestine)
**Aim.** Help the Grid free the unregistered from the orders of the CVD and the Tower families.

**How it opens.** Earn **Tanizaki's** trust (Gin's 30-year mentor, the door to the Grid); a cash reading or a safehouse favour; Kuroda Sei's grievance; the rotating **Gathering** through **Switch**.

**Key NPCs.** Tanizaki (the gate, the ward methodology), Switch (the unmappable meet), Choi Hana (the free Arbiter), Majima/Tanaka/Kurosawa/Uehara (the master independents), Hara Setsu / the bath-house network, Kuroki (organised crime, a terrifying ally).

**Beats.** Trust → the Gathering → fully inside the network → the Grid's own coercion question.

**Ending trigger — the Grid's nature resolves.**
- **Noble.** A real underground that keeps the unseen safe and self-governing; trust paid in action.
- **Dark.** The Grid becomes another power with its own coercions; liberation curdles into a different cage.
- **Tragedy.** A sweep, a betrayal, a debt called in; the people who survive by being unseen are made very findable.

## 3. The Two-Demon Endgame

The player is caught between two demons who want opposite things:

| | **Yushiro** (Duty · XIII, in Gin) | **Kishin / the Inverted-God aspirant** (Far-Shore Circle) |
|---|---|---|
| **Wants** | To discharge an interrupted duty; will drop the veil and merge living + dead to do it | To feed and ascend to the Inverted God — the dark mirror of the Axis |
| **Re: power** | Does **not** want power or the Axis | Wants to overturn the order entirely |
| **Re: the other** | Hunting the aspirant, trying to kill it before it ascends | Being hunted; if it wins, open war |
| **If it wins** | The veil drops | War between the 13 demons and the 13 strongest mediums |

**The trap.** Defeating Yushiro can hand the board to something far hungrier. Outcomes range from a clean exorcism, to a devil's alliance (let Yushiro win to save Gin), to becoming the cult's successor.

## 5. The Endings matrix (how one fires first)

Each road's central variable resolves into one of three. The **earliest to resolve fires the campaign ending** and can foreclose the others.

| Road | Central variable | Noble | Dark | Tragic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Thirteen/Axis** | The Duty seat resolves | Seat held well, Kita steadied, Gin freed | Seat held corrupted, power over balance | Too late/wrong; Gin dies or Rei dissolves |
| **B. CVD** | Rei's standing resolves | Reform from within | The institution's instrument | Ground down / scapegoated |
| **C. Tower** | The patronage resolves | Real shelter, reach without a leash | A family's enforcer | Spent like a debt, discarded |
| **D. Brother/Cult** | Possession + cult resolve | Gin freed, war averted | Cult's successor / veil dropped | Gin dies / the war begins |
| **E. Grid** | The Grid's nature resolves | Safe, self-governing underground | Another cage | Sweep / betrayal / debt called in |

**Design rule.** The Gin thread is present in every column — even the CVD/Tower/Grid endings resolve *something* about Gin (freed, filed away, used as leverage, or lost). No road lets the player forget the brother who is the reason.
