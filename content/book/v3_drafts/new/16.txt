#title A Small Light

*The Font.*

There is light, and the light is working.

It has always been working. It does not remember starting. It does not think about stopping. It holds, and it feeds, and it keeps the thin skin between the living and the dead pulled tight. That is all it knows of itself. Nobody has ever asked it what it is.

Tonight something comes through.

First, an old grey one, tired and slow, going home at last. That happens. The light lets it pass, as it lets them all pass, and barely notices.

But behind the old one, something else. Something small. Something that should not have been able to come so far. A little light, steady and stubborn, that came through holding the door open for the old one and did not stop in time.

The light notices the little one.

The little light stops at the edge of everything, very small, very far away, and asks it a question. Sometimes they do. Once in a long while, someone is pushed through the white, half drowned, and asks something. The light answers. It is one of the things it does. Then it sends them back, because the living cannot stay here.

This little light asks about a brother.

Not about itself, though it is very close to not living, closer than it knows. It has one question, and it spends it on somebody else, without stopping to think.

The light looks for the brother. He is not hard to find. Something is wearing him. Something old and patient, with a weight in it that the light knows.

*Yes,* says the light. *He is alive.*

It sends the little one home.

Then it goes back to the work, and the work closes over it again, the way it always does.

But something is different, and the light does not know what. A small steady warmth at the very edge of everything, that was not there before.

The little light is still walking toward it. Very far away. Very small. Walking.
