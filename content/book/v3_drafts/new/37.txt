#title The Light Comes Closer

*The Font.*

The little light is closer now.

The Font feels it as you feel the sun move across a room with your eyes shut. One side a little warmer than before. The little one has not come through the white again since the morning of the old man and his fish shop. But it is nearer. It walks in the city above, small and steady, and every week it is a little brighter.

It has other lights round it now. Small ones.

One that has a sleeping thing curled up inside it, locked in behind a closed door with many locks, and does not seem afraid of it.

One that hears everything, and keeps a small hard piece of something in its pocket, and is a little less alone than it was.

One very old and very patient, in a garden gone to seed, that the light has felt from far away for many years.

One with notebooks. One with a bath full of warm water and somebody sitting in the corner of it. One with a crow.

The light does not know why it notices them. It does not notice the small lights round any one person. It does not look at small things. It holds, and it feeds, and it keeps the skin tight, and that is all.

It notices these.

[break The gap]

At the edge of everything there is a place where the skin gives when it is pressed. A gap. A place where something should be and is not.

The light has always known about the gap. It does not think about it. It works round it, every moment, the way water works round a stone.

But now, when the little light moves in the city, the gap moves too. Very slightly. As if it has noticed something it recognises.

The light does not know what that means. It does not want to know. Knowing is not the work.

It goes back to the work.

[break Waiting]

And yet.

Deep down, under the work, in a place the light did not know it had, something has changed. It is very small. It is not a thought. It is not a memory. It is only a direction. The light, which has never faced any way at all, finds that it is facing one way now. Toward the small bright thing walking in the city with a crow and a boy who draws doors.

It is waiting.

It has not waited for anything in a very long time.

It does not mind.
