Update Log

v0.7 · current
The Doorways

A front door, three ways in, and a phone that finally lets you out.

  • Landing page with three modes — Play the Lore Game (live), AI GM Campaign and Group Campaign (coming soon) — plus a playable-cast showcase and a glimpse of the world.
  • Age gate & maturity modes: an 18+ / under-18 check on launch that gates the darker content; adult horror is unrestrained, under-18 stays restrained.
  • Mobile fix: the in-game phone now closes cleanly with a tap-bar, a swipe, or a tap outside — no more getting trapped.
v0.6
The Centre

The campaign found its ending.

  • The Axis: a closeness meter and the four routes that draw you toward the thing at the centre, the L5 talk, and five endings — the good road, the rescue, and the dark ones.
  • The Two Thirteens cosmology and the Five Roads (CVD, Towers, the Grid, the cult, the Axis) authored into the Warden's book.
  • The dark path: take in what you defeat, and slowly become an absorber yourself.
  • A living news feed that seeds the trail toward the missing as the days pass.
v0.5
The Living World

The city stopped waiting for you.

  • NPCs as agents: they remember, gossip, follow through on promises, and act offscreen.
  • The thirteen emotions on every character — the register a spirit could ride.
  • Death & consequence: people can die or vanish for good (off the map, a silent chat-log), the wounded recover in the CVD Health Center, and those who loved them grieve.
  • Party: companions fight beside you (talk a foe down, or fall them faster together), share the XP, and ride along in the top bar.
  • Personal & side quests with a place, a stake, and a real consequence.
v0.4
The Memory & the Mind

Foundations under everything above.

  • A memory engine the Warden reads and archives, so the world stays consistent across a long game.
  • Deep characters: a full inner-life layer (intellect, wounds, vices, tastes) on all of the cast.
  • Rank, registration & abilities synced across the book, the data, and the game.
