Warden’s Eyes Only · Campaign Reference
NPC Homes & Routines
Where every named NPC lives, works, and can be found — by night and on a Saturday. Place names in amber link to the gazetteer (Gatherings / Resources / Disturbed / Official). Red = GM secret. Homes are GM-side; players learn them by working for them.

The CVD & the Regulated

Salaried, registered, or institutionally entangled. Most keep regular hours; the building is the spine of their week.

Nakamura Aoi 中村 葵
CVD · Field Operations
Lives
Kita-ku, a small one-room flat two minutes from the Saikyō line, plants she forgets to water.
Works
CVD Field Operations floor — the desk nearest the door, more nights than not.
Most nights
Still at the office past midnight, or decompressing at Beck’s Stand, Platform 3 before the last train.
Saturdays
Visits her mother in Saitama; if a case is open, she comes in anyway and pretends she didn’t.
The easiest person in the building to let help you. Talks with her hands; fills silences before they turn heavy.

The three the campaign turns on. Gin’s routine is the one to watch — since the possession it has begun, quietly, to slip.

The Thirteen — Where to Find Them

Canon locations from the Domain Registry, placed into the gazetteer and tied to the people already there. The holders are GM secrets; players meet them as ordinary strangers in these spots long before they learn what they hold.

I · Grief — Asakura Mio 朝倉 美緒
Holder · Kyoto · Stable · (worked-example sheet exists)
Found at
Off the Tokyo map — Kyoto, reached only by referral through the Grid. Not a walk-in.
Tied to
The grief-readers know her name: Choi Hana, and through her, the riverside keeper Mrs. Endō.
II · Rage — Mori Takao 森 高雄
Holder · Tokyo (hard to find) · Under pressure
Found at
Surfaces in the Disturbed Areas — the freight-yard blocks, the fight rooms behind the pachinko. He does not stay anywhere.
Tied to
Old Tomi has watched him cross the freight-yard fence and not understood what he saw.
III · Love — Sasaki Kei 佐々木 慧
Holder · Tokyo · Stable
Found at
Where people are gentle with each other — a dawn regular at Akebono Bakery, and the quiet end of the host clubs.
Tied to
Mrs. Kawai the baker feeds them without knowing; Leo the host has felt oddly, helplessly honest near them.
IV · Fear — Nakashima Yuna 中島 由奈
Holder · Tokyo · Under pressure
Found at
The thin, frightening places — the station underpass, the worst corners after dark.
Tied to
The blind busker has heard her pass and packed up early. The Suzuran 2am woman feels her coming.
V · Wonder — Chen Lian 陈莲
Holder · Tokyo (Grid Archive) · Stable
Found at
The Private Occult Library / the Grid Archive — she is effectively its keeper.
Tied to
The collector and Yamamoto Koji both defer to her without quite knowing why; Master Lin respects her.
VI · Despair — Watanabe Jun 渡辺 順
Holder · Variable · Weakening
Found at
The Riverside Memorial and the hospital wards — wherever despair pools. Hard to pin; he is fading.
Tied to
Mrs. Endō has felt him at the water on the bad anniversaries and mistaken him for a mourner.
VII · Joy — Hoshino Haru 星野 晴
Holder · Kita, Sakae St · Stable · your “ramen-shop one of the Thirteen”
Found at
Tatsuya, the late ramen counter under the Sakae St tracks — and the warm Kita corners generally.
Tied to
Eats two stools from Moriya; Tatsu the master knows the regular’s face, not the truth. The warmest of the Thirteen, hidden in the warmest place.
VIII · Shame — Kobayashi Emi 小林 恵美
Holder · Tokyo · Stable
Found at
The places people go to hide — the back pew of Father Anselm’s church after hours, the Peony’s strip.
Tied to
Father Anselm has taken her confession and felt the floor tilt; Mariko has seen her drift through.
IX · Hunger — Inoue Satoru 井上 悟
Holder · No fixed location · Under pressure
Found at
Roams the nightlife, feeding — Vault, the host clubs, the after-hours rooms. Never the same place twice.
Tied to
Endō feels him come up the stair and never knows why his skin crawls. Aizawa is exactly his kind of prey.
X · Courage — Fujiwara Natsumi 藤原 夏美
Holder · Independent · Stable
Found at
The Tower Dojo and the training grounds — she teaches, quietly, those who seek it.
Tied to
The lineage instructors know her as a guest master; Tanaka Ryu has trained under her once and never forgotten it.
XI · Longing — Park Junho 박준호
Holder · Shin-Ōkubo · Under pressure
Found at
The Korean quarter — the mudang’s street, the diaspora houses, the all-night corners of Shin-Ōkubo.
Tied to
Pak the mudang knows exactly what he is and keeps his secret; Choi Hana grew up two doors from him.
XII · Awe — Kasumi Shin 霞 慎
Holder · Variable · Stable
Found at
The sacred grounds — the Shinto shrine, the temples, wherever the air goes still.
Tied to
Miyamoto the kannushi knows when he has visited; the salt at the gate is disturbed and no one came through it.
XIII · Duty — VACANT since 2009 空席
Kita district · CRITICAL · the answer is Rei
Found at
The Uedera flat, Kita-ku — asleep on the couch, doing homework, oblivious. The vacant seat, and the boy who fills it.
Tied to
Everyone in Gin’s life, and the membrane thinning over Kita while the seat stays empty.

On existing people in the places (don’t double-book): Toraya → Tanizaki; Tatsuya → Moriya + Tatsu + Hoshino/Joy; the cat that keeps Inoue Misaki untraceable; the Grid Archive → Chen Lian/Wonder. New location-staff (bartenders, keepers, witnesses) sit alongside these, never on top of them.

25 named NPCs + the 13 holders placed = the full cast located. Homes and routines are GM-side and adjustable — change any of them; the structure is built to take it. Place names tie back to the gazetteer so a night’s “who’s where” is a two-minute lookup.
