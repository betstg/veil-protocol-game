NPC Homes & Distances
Where every named character lives, who they live with, and how to get there from the CVD — on foot or by a named, real train line. Zero point: the HQ beside Ōji Station (王子), Kita-ku.
The anchor — Ōji Station (王子), Kita-ku. A real station: JR Keihin-Tōhoku Line, Tokyo Metro Namboku Line, and the Toden Arakawa tram. The CVD building stands one minute from the east exit, on the fault-line intersection at the centre of the district’s thin spots. Every “by train” below is a real route from Ōji — the line to take is named.
Who lives together — the analysis. Among named NPCs, only Gin & Rei share a home (the two-room Kita-ku flat). Everyone else lives alone, with family, or shares with unnamed people: Shimizu Ren, Kurosawa Shou, Mochizuki Sora, Wada Sho, and ‘Mali’. No other named pair cohabits. (Usami & Ren share a body, not a flat.)
