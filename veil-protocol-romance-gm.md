# Veil Protocol — Romance Routes: "How to Win Them" (GM Book, Block 2)

*Romance is a deliberate route the player chooses, never automatic. Attraction is earned and can be lost; a clumsy or mistimed advance moves it DOWN. Each route has a noble, a dark, and a tragic end. **Hard line: never romance, flirt with, or sexualize a minor under any framing.** Mori (17) and Yua (16) are crush-only — characterisation, not a route.*


## The 25 romanceable adults

### Usami Hinata / Ren
**Opens:** Player deliberately chooses the romance route after the Pair trusts Rei enough to let him see the seam.
**Beats:**
- Attraction lands doubled — you are courting two, and they do not always agree on you.
- Ren’s register makes intimacy frighteningly literal: it wants to be known, fully, and offers the same.
- Usami needs to hear he is still wanted as himself, not as the interesting condition.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Rei loves both and helps them hold the seam — a partnership that models survival for Gin.
- **Dark —** Rei lets Ren’s pull do the work; easy, total, and quietly erasing Usami.
- **Tragic —** The contract fails mid-relationship and Rei must choose which of the two to try to save.

### Kuroda Itsuki
**Opens:** Player chooses the route; Itsuki is an adult and flirts first, but reciprocates seriously only once Rei stops performing for him.
**Beats:**
- Banter is the easy tier — he gives it to strangers; warmth he rations.
- He paints Rei before he’ll talk to Rei; the portrait says what he won’t.
- Asking him to use his sight on Gin is asking him to betray the family silence — the real intimacy is that he does it.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Itsuki chooses Rei over the Kuroda silence and becomes a witness who finally tells the truth.
- **Dark —** Rei uses the flirtation to work the Kuroda family and hollows out the one honest thing Itsuki offered.
- **Tragic —** The family forces a side; loving Rei is what gets Itsuki noticed, and noticed is not safe.

### Hagiwara Mizuki
**Opens:** Player pursues it; Mizuki, an adult, allows it only once she trusts Rei with what she actually thinks, not what she says.
**Beats:**
- She performs warmth professionally; the route is teaching her to mean it with one person.
- Every confidence she gives him is a risk to a thirty-year discipline.
- Intimacy is her letting Rei see the cost of the lies she keeps.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Mizuki lets one true thing exist outside the machine, and protects Rei for real.
- **Dark —** Rei becomes another managed asset she happens to sleep with.
- **Tragic —** Loving Rei makes her choose between him and the cover-up she has given her life to.

### Nakamura Aoi
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Aoi is an adult, earnest, and falls a little fast — the route is about whether Rei is careful with that.
**Beats:**
- Her admiration is real and slightly hero-shaped; she has to see the tired person under it.
- She is brave for him before she's wise for herself.
- Reciprocation means Rei treating her heart as carefully as her loyalty deserves.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** They grow up alongside each other; she becomes a partner, not a follower.
- **Dark —** Rei lets her adoration carry the relationship and never meets her as an equal.
- **Tragic —** Her loyalty to Rei puts her in front of something a second-year can't survive.

### Kato Ryusei
**Opens:** Player chooses it; for a telepath, being chosen rather than predicted is the whole point — he reciprocates only when Rei stays opaque enough to mean it.
**Beats:**
- He braces for the disappointment he always hears coming; the route is it not coming.
- He can read intention but not feeling — Rei has to say things out loud.
- Intimacy is Ryusei trusting a closeness he cannot verify by scanning.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** He learns to be surprised, and stops living one half-second ahead of his own life.
- **Dark —** Rei uses the Tower bloodline and the telepathy, and confirms every cynicism Ryusei ever held.
- **Tragic —** The Tower wants its son back, and the one person he couldn't read becomes the leverage against him.

### Katagiri Noa
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Noa is an adult, guarded by grief — the route only opens once Rei is loved for himself, not as Gin's echo.
**Beats:**
- The resemblance to Gin is the obstacle, not the draw; she has to want Rei, not the ghost.
- Her grief came first and will not be rushed.
- Intimacy is her letting a new thing exist without it erasing the old one.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Noa grieves Gin and chooses Rei cleanly, two true things instead of one substituting the other.
- **Dark —** Rei lets her see Gin in him and takes the love meant for a dead man.
- **Tragic —** Saving Gin and loving Noa pull opposite ways, and she cannot have both intact.

### Moriya Tatsuki
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Moriya, an adult, does not mix work and feeling easily — the route is what it costs a process-man to make an exception.
**Beats:**
- He audits the relationship like everything else until he can't.
- Choosing Rei means choosing a person over the institution he's built his integrity on.
- Intimacy is the watcher letting himself be seen.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Moriya finds the line where principle serves a person, and protects Rei without breaking himself.
- **Dark —** He bends every process for Rei and becomes the corruption he used to hunt.
- **Tragic —** The institution forces him to choose, and integrity and love can't both survive the choice.

### Okada Shizuka
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Okada is an adult whose loyalty to Gin came first — the route is her wanting Rei for Rei, not as a duty discharged.
**Beats:**
- She started on his side for Gin's sake; she has to stay for her own.
- Her steadiness hides how long she's been alone with this.
- Intimacy is the keeper of the promise letting herself be the one who's wanted.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Okada's loyalty matures from Gin's instruction into her own freely-given love.
- **Dark —** Rei leans on the promise she made Gin and never has to earn her himself.
- **Tragic —** What she owes Gin and what she feels for Rei diverge when Gin's fate is decided.

### Shimizu Ren
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Shimizu, an adult, lets almost no one past the discipline — the route is the suppressor allowing her own breach.
**Beats:**
- She answers what Rei was about to ask, and never says what she herself needs.
- Closeness with Gin's brother reopens a grief she's holding shut.
- Intimacy is the woman who never flinches letting herself, once, flinch.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Shimizu lets the line down for one person and discovers she survives it.
- **Dark —** Rei takes the trust meant for Gin and gives nothing of his own back.
- **Tragic —** Holding the line for the exorcism and opening to Rei demand opposite things of her at the worst moment.

### Tendo Akira
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Tendo, an adult, flirts easily and means little of it until Rei is the thing he can't charm past.
**Beats:**
- The easy charm is armour; the route is reaching the operative under it.
- Two anomalies recognising each other is its own dangerous intimacy.
- Loving Rei is the first consequence Tendo's privilege can't pre-empt.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Tendo stops outrunning himself and stands with Rei as what he actually is.
- **Dark —** Rei uses the family network and the recognition, and lets Tendo stay a charming stranger to himself.
- **Tragic —** When both anomalies become visible at once, the network that shielded him is the leash that pulls him away.

### Tsukishiro Haruki
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Haruki, an adult, theorises before he feels — the route is him letting the feeling outrun the theory.
**Beats:**
- He understands Rei intellectually long before he risks anything emotionally.
- Shared obsession is the easy intimacy; ordinary closeness is the hard one.
- Reciprocation is Haruki setting the file down to actually be present.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** He learns to be with someone, not just study them, and the loneliness finally lifts.
- **Dark —** Rei becomes the most interesting case he ever worked, and never quite a person to him.
- **Tragic —** The file was always pointing somewhere fatal, and loving Rei means following it to the end.

### Choi Hana
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Hana, an adult of Rei's own age, lets almost no one close — the route is armour coming off in a life that punished softness.
**Beats:**
- Her hostility is a test she half-hopes you'll fail so it stays simple.
- Same age, opposite circumstances — she envies and resents the cover Rei has.
- Intimacy is the unregistered girl risking the one thing she can't take back: trust.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Two nineteen-year-olds choose each other against worlds built to isolate them.
- **Dark —** Rei mines her Grid and her rank and proves every reason she had not to trust.
- **Tragic —** Loving a registered intern is the exposure a free Arbiter cannot survive.

### Kurosawa Shou
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Kurosawa, an adult, hides warmth under graveyard humour — the route is reaching the kindness he buries.
**Beats:**
- His levity about death is armour; grief-work has cost him more than he admits.
- He reads bodies but guards his own; closeness unnerves the diagnostician.
- Intimacy is the man who touches the dead letting himself be touched by the living.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Kurosawa lets the kindness surface and stops working alone among corpses.
- **Dark —** Rei uses his gift to read Gin and never sees the man behind the diagnosis.
- **Tragic —** What he reads in Gin's body is also what he fears for Rei, and loving him means watching it come.

### Inoue Misaki
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Inoue, an adult, is sure she'll only ever be wanted for her power — the route is being wrong about that.
**Beats:**
- She braces for Rei to want the Null Space, not her; the route is him wanting her.
- Singularity isolation has made closeness theoretical; she's out of practice at being a person.
- Intimacy is the last resort discovering she is also a first choice.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Inoue is needed as herself and rejoins the human scale she'd left.
- **Dark —** Rei takes the Null Space and confirms her belief that the rank is all anyone wants.
- **Tragic —** The case that finally clears her threshold is the one that takes everything to win.

### Tanaka Ryu
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Tanaka, an adult, steadies everyone and accepts nothing back — the route is letting himself be the one held.
**Beats:**
- He shows up for Rei the way he shows up for everyone; the route is it becoming specific.
- Being needed is comfortable for him; being wanted is the frightening part.
- Intimacy is the steady hand admitting it shakes too.
**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Tanaka learns to lean, and the reliable man finally gets held up.

- **Tragic —** He steadies Rei through the one operation that he himself doesn't walk out of.

### Majima Kenji
**Opens:** Player chooses it; Majima, an adult, has walled off softness to protect what little he has — the route is the wall coming down once.
**Beats:**
- The gruffness is real and also a fence around the daughter and everything tender.

**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Majima lets Rei past the wall and stops carrying it all alone.
- **Dark —** Rei buys the muscle and never earns the man, and the wall stays up for good reason.

### Uehara

**Beats:**
- He cannot bear to be pitied; affection has to come as respect, not rescue.

**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Uehara is loved as himself, not his decline, and the last years are not lonely ones.

### Mochizuki Sora

**Beats:**
- Recognition is instant; trust is slow, because being believed is the unfamiliar thing.

**Where it can go:**
- **Noble —** Two solitary investigators become genuine partners, in the work and out of it.
- **Dark —** Rei merges her years of work into his and leaves her alone again, used.

## Crush — one-sided, NO romance (minors)

### Mori Haruto *(minor — crush only)*
- **After:** A genuine friendship is established — Mori has stood by Rei through something real.
- **Tone:** One-sided, unspoken or gently deflected. Rei is kind and clear; Mori is not mocked for it.
- **Ceiling:** A promise to talk ‘when you’re older’ and a hug. No romance, no abilities, no more. This line does not move.

### Yua *(minor — crush only)*
- **After:** Rei proves, more than once, that he is not going to use her or vanish.
- **Tone:** One-sided and unspoken. Played as loyalty and bravado, never reciprocated romantically.
- **Ceiling:** She gets a reliable adult who shows up. That is the whole arc, and it matters. No romance, ever.
