LORE ARCHIVE
KUROSAWA CITY

A mid-sized Japanese coastal city where the veil between the physical world and something older has grown thin over three years. The city's infrastructure looks normal. Its shadows do not behave normally.

Three years ago: unexplained disappearances, equipment failures in the old ward, and a documented uptick in what the Directorate calls "anomalous social behavior" near the waterbank district.

THE VEIL

What practitioners call the membrane between ordinary space and the layer beneath it where entities move. The Veil is not a place. It is a property of space. Some locations have almost no veil left. Others are thicker than they should be.

Gin's research suggests the Veil thins through collective belief, repeated ritual, or the sustained presence of certain entities. It does not self-repair quickly.

STABILITY

Rei's capacity to interpret what she sees within normal psychological frameworks. Starts at 100. Drops with entity exposure, traumatic revelation, and certain choices. At 0, Rei cannot distinguish the city from what is beneath it. Some abilities only unlock at low stability.

MADNESS

Distinct from Stability. Madness measures how much the other side has marked Rei. Madness can open doors Stability cannot. High Madness with high Stability is possible. It is an unstable condition.

CLAIRSENTIENCE

Rei's latent ability to read psychic residue on objects and locations. Five levels. Each level unlocks scene fragments and choice options not otherwise visible. At level 3 and above, it begins operating without Rei's intention.

THE FIVE ROADS

Five primary investigation paths. Roads overlap. Choices on one road affect what is visible on others. The endings depend on combinations, not individual paths.

READERS AND DEAD READERS

A reader is a Veil-sensitive individual who can perceive entities and residue. A dead reader is a reader whose sensitivity overloaded and who is now locked in a permanent perceptual state. They are not dead. They are unreachable by normal means.
