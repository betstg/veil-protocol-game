Field Notes on Grounding Objects

A grounding object accumulates frequency signature over time. How much depends on how long you carry it and how much you have gone through while carrying it.

Years one and two, the signature is low. A practitioner at CL Level 4 or above can read the basic emotional register of the owner with focused effort. Not operationally sensitive.

Years three through five, the signature reaches moderate. CL Level 3 can access major emotional events in the practitioner's history. Ability type and approximate level become detectable. At this stage the object is beginning to constitute an intelligence risk.

Six years and beyond, the signature is high. CL Level 2 can read a full practitioner profile at contact range. Rank estimate possible. Network connections implied. A grounding object carried for six or more years is a complete intelligence document in object form.

This is why experienced practitioners do not leave their grounding objects in the field.

The keychain I carry has been in my possession for six years.

Rei now has the spare.

If something has happened to me, treat it as sensitive material.

Soul-Vessels

When a spirit chooses the object instead of the Font.

Every spirit that crosses has the same moment. The membrane opens. The Font is there. What the Font offers is completion — dissolution into the structure, the specific peace of no longer having to be a self. Most spirits take it. Most spirits do not understand there is another option.

The option is this. At the moment of crossing, a spirit can choose an object instead of the Font. Not any object. An object they loved, or one that loved them back through the hands of someone they cared about.

What I Carry — Field Notes on Symbol Work

I carry six prepared configurations at any time. Three containment variants. I adjust which three depending on the expected entity type. One banishment. One communication, for the spirit encounters where talking is the right approach. One that I developed myself in year four, a slow-acting disruption configuration, which I keep having to re-explain to Grid contacts who find it on me because it does not look like anything in their standard reference.

I prepare everything in advance. I have never successfully prepared a configuration in the field under active threat. I have tried twice. Both times I used what I had already made, and both times the situation resolved or did not resolve on the basis of what was already in my kit, not what I attempted to draw while something was actively trying to get into my perimeter.

Prepare in advance. This is the only advice on symbol work that matters before you learn the rest.

The configuration I use most often is the threshold ward. I have a standard set pre-drawn and folded in the left front pocket of every bag I own. I replace them weekly whether or not I have used them because I do not want to reach for one and find it degraded.

The most powerful thing I carry is the exorcism circle, in five parts, sealed separately. I have used it once in six years. I prepared for it for three months before the operation. The preparation made the difference.

Skin Glyphs — Permanent Activation Warning

Full documentation. Read before any permanent application.

Every glyph in the glyph dictionary can be tattooed on skin. The ink substrate works. The configuration is permanent. This is where the comparison with paper glyphs ends entirely.

Type State Activation
Paper glyph Dormant until used Activated by intent. Off until you turn it on.
Skin glyph (untrained) Always active Interacting with the membrane continuously. No off switch.

In high-entity environments, the skin glyph responds to entity presence automatically, activating fully without the practitioner choosing it. In emotionally intense situations, skin glyphs can surge. A practitioner with an untrained Restraining Circle tattoo who experiences acute emotional distress may spontaneously activate a full containment field around themselves. This traps them inside their own glyph.

The cascade risk. Multiple skin glyphs interact. Two glyphs that function correctly in isolation create interference when both activate simultaneously. The anti-possession glyph and the Banishment glyph tattooed on the same person will attempt to fire together in a possession event. The containment effect and the expulsion effect conflict at the point of activation, producing neither correctly and costing the practitioner full backlash from both.

This is not theoretical. The Grid has documented cases.

The Field Kit

Location

Tanizaki safe house, third cabinet. Ask before using.

The Object

A leather field surgery kit from the 1920s, containing instruments in original configuration. Belonged to a practitioner-medic who died in the 1923 Kanto earthquake performing Psychometabolism-level emergency stabilization on thirty-seven people in six hours, until she ran out of stability.

The instruments remember the work.

What It Does

Opening the kit stabilizes every living consciousness in the room. Practitioners, civilians, partially possessed hosts. Twenty points. Immediate. Does not discriminate between who should be stabilized and who should not.

Entities in partial-physical form in the room also stabilize. An entity that is actively destabilizing gets a reprieve. This has been used to negotiate with entities mid-clearance. It has also been used to keep possessed hosts alive long enough for an exorcism to reach them.

The Cost

Stabilizing the entity stabilizes it. A clearance operation that uses the kit to keep the host alive also makes the clearance harder. The entity is no longer at the depletion point where it would otherwise be vulnerable. Choose carefully.

Opening — 3:47am

3:47am. The kitchen light is on and Gin is not in it.

You find the keychain on the walkway outside. The spare key on the hook beside the door — the one he has had for six years, that you have not touched, that you have been pretending is just a key — is gone. He left the Archive open on the table.

You do not know if he meant for you to find it or if it was open when he left.

You read the first page. Then the second. Then you understand what you are holding.

Gin kept records. Twenty-eight years old, Vanguard-grade, and he kept records of everything — the cases, the contacts, the things the CVD was not supposed to know about, the things the Grid would not put in writing. Six years of notes in a handwriting that is very clean until the fourteenth entry.

You pick up the keychain. You go outside.

There are five roads from here.

The Oji Overpass

The city at 4am is not empty. It is just a different city. The konbini on Showa-dori, the salaryman standing outside it with the cigarette that is his only breathing room. The bikes chained to the overpass rail. The sound of the Kanda river running under Oji station.

You stand on the overpass. The air here has a quality to it that the street did not. Something in the membrane, close to the surface. The thin spot under the southbound platform is documented in Gin's notes, Class E, stable since 1998. You can feel why he documented it. Even standing here it is possible to understand that this is a place where something could pass through if conditions were right.

They are not right tonight. Tonight it is just air. But you feel the potential of it.

The Lingering

The old man at the overpass railing. White hair, canvas jacket that should be too cold for this. He is not cold. He has not been cold for a long time.

He is looking south. Not at anything specific. The direction of something that was once south of here and is not anymore.

If you have the ability, you can feel what he is. A D-grade lingering. Not dangerous. Barely present. He is so much of what he always was that he simply continued, the way some things continue past the point when they should stop, not from force but from habit.

You have the choice to help him or leave him. If you help him, he looks at you. He does not speak, but his face does something that means something to you, something about being seen at 4am when you did not know you needed it.

The Five Roads

There are five roads from the overpass. You do not have to take all of them. You may not have the time to take all of them. But each one leads somewhere Gin went, and each one will tell you something about what has happened to him. The thing they will not tell you is where he is now. That is something you will have to find yourself, at the end, when you know enough to know where to look.

Kuroda Itsuki is at the Geidai studio at 4am because he is always at the studio at 4am. He sees something wrong on you the moment you walk in. Not wrong like threat. Wrong like a painting where the light source is impossible. He cannot stop looking.

Road Four — The Reach

You reach for the thing you are not supposed to do. The room tilts. For half a second you feel the cold of the key and a hand that set it down, a fear not yours... then it is gone and your head is ringing. Minus twelve stability.

Clairsentience in the flat. Reading your own reach. You were not supposed to be able to do this. Whatever the CVD file says about your abilities, whatever Gin told them, whatever you believed about yourself until tonight — the key is Gin's and you felt him set it down and what he was feeling when he did and it was not fear of the thing following him. It was something more complicated than fear.

The Thirteen's offer. They have been watching the succession line for nine years. They know what Rei is. The offer is this. Let them help. The cost of the offer is that you understand what you actually are before you are ready to.

Refusing with Gin's key. You take the key and walk north. The Axis-pull makes sense now in a way it did not before.

4am city walk. Suzuran bar in Ni-chome. Kaneda Toru has been open since before the district was safe to be open in. He has seen every kind of person the city produces at 4am and he does not ask you why you are here. He pours something and you sit at the bar and he does not fill the silence.

The old man on the way home. The one you either helped at the overpass or passed. If you helped him, something in the district knows you were here. Not a threat. A witness. The dead in Kita know Gin, and one of them has just decided they know you.

The riverbank at Obon. The dead are facing north. All of them, every lingering presence in the district, oriented toward the same point. Not the Font. Something else. A pull that should not exist.

Domain XIII — Full Record

Warden-only document. Contains structural spoilers. Do not distribute to players before the campaign's final act.

The Keystone

Everything stable depends on it, and no one holds it.

Duty is the quality of persisting in a function because stopping would cost everything. Not obedience. Not loyalty. The specific quality of continuing because discontinuation is not survivable by those who depend on the continuation.

It is the least glamorous of the thirteen registers and the one that cannot be vacant. Every other domain can thin and produce difficult entities and be managed. A thin Duty register thins the entire structure. The Axis absorbs the load. The Axis has been absorbing it for seventeen years.

Why It Was Misclassified as Vacant

CVD instruments are calibrated for variation. Duty's total stillness reads as null. They recorded fullness as nothing. The instruments were not wrong. They were measuring the wrong axis.

The Suppression Problem

Gin's official grade is 3d6 Ritualist. His actual grade is 6d6 Vanguard. Suppression Level 4 is required to maintain that discrepancy in an official assessment. Most practitioners cannot suppress past Level 2. Gin developed Level 4 suppression specifically to stay under CVD threshold for six years while doing work the CVD was not supposed to know about.

Rei's official grade is D-rank. Actual rank Arbiter approaching Vanguard. The CVD instruments read her multi-spectrum capacity as noise because the instruments are not calibrated for that pattern. This is not suppression. The instruments are simply measuring the wrong thing.

The Three Structures

CVD — Clairvoyant Veil Division

The government body. Official classification, licensing, clearance operations. Theoretically comprehensive. In practice, the CVD knows what practitioners report to it and what it can measure with instruments calibrated against a standard the instruments defined.

Hagiwara Mizuki's Closing project is CVD initiative. The CVD does not know about Domain XIII. Its instruments have been recording the vacancy as null since 2009.

The Grid — Independent Network

Operates in Sanya, Minami-Senju, and adjacent working-class districts. Practitioners who have decided, for various reasons, that CVD registration is not something they will be doing. The Grid does not have clearance authority. It has operational reach the CVD cannot replicate because the CVD requires practitioners to be visible.

Gin maintained Grid contacts without the CVD's knowledge for six years. The Grid knew something was wrong with him before the CVD file was opened.

The Tower — Old Families

The Tower is not a place. It is a shorthand for the old practitioner families who have been watching this city for enough generations that the watching has become constitutional. The Kuroda family is Tower-adjacent. The Tower proper consists of six families who predate the CVD by several centuries.

Tower knowledge about the Thirteen is significant and specific. What the Tower fears about Domain XIII is not the vacancy. It is succession by someone outside Tower influence.

The Possession Track

Not a binary state. A gradual accumulation of entity influence over the host's decision-making, then emotional register, then will, then identity. The host remains present at every stage. They can often describe what is happening to them. That capacity to describe it diminishes as the track progresses.

Gin at campaign start is at approximately Stage 4. The handwriting change in the Archive represents the Stage 3 to Stage 4 transition. Stage 5 is the point where the host can no longer access their own intentions clearly enough to distinguish them from the entity's.

The lucid window is Stage 4's residual self-awareness. It closes at Stage 5.
