The Pitch... story & product, in one read

The whole thing, the way you'd present it. Read top to bottom; each block is a beat of the pitch.

Veil Protocol: The Other Side
A haunted Tokyo casebook you can read... and then step inside.
Find your brother before the dead thing wearing him finishes the job.
01 · The World
Tokyo, 2015. The dead never fully left.

They press against a thinning membrane called the veil, and they want back in... most of all the ones lost without a grave, after the disaster of 2011. A quiet government bureau, the CVD, exists to keep them on the far side and to license the few living people who can sense them. The city looks ordinary. It is not.

02 · The Hook
Your brother is gone, and he left an archive.

Uedera Gin... a CVD operative... vanishes. What he leaves behind is a thousand-page case archive: everything he knew about the veil, written for someone who would one day come looking. You are that someone. What the archive can't tell you is that Gin didn't run. He was taken... from the inside.

03 · Who You Are
Rei... the brother who keeps Gin here.

You are Uedera Rei: not CVD, not Gin's partner... his brother. A criminal-investigation intern by day, sharing a cramped Kita-ku flat where Gin's shoes are still by the couch. The Uedera line runs deep, but yours is suppressed and dormant... until Gin disappears and it starts to wake. The first thing you'll do in the game is look in a mirror and finally recognise your own face. The secret the story is built on: your nearness is what anchors Gin to himself. Looking for him isn't just love. It's the only thing keeping him alive.

04 · The Three Powers
A door that won't close, a door welded shut, and you between them.

Yushiro... the demon wearing Gin. Not a monster: a long-dead healer who simply refused to stay dead. He's using Gin's body to hunt something worse, and every day he wears him, Gin rots. He wants the veil open.

Kishin... the true villain. A top-grade demon who hollowed out a clairsentient woman and runs a grief cult, the Far-Shore Circle, farming despair to make himself a god... to raise hell on the human side and crown it.

The PR Director... inside the CVD. She wants to cage every medium and weld the veil shut by force... which traps the good, the evil, and everything between. She isn't wrong that mediums are dangerous, which is what makes her terrifying.

Yushiro and Kishin are two of thirteen high-grade demons... one for every emotion. Yushiro holds Duty (asleep inside Gin); Kishin is the evil one. The other eleven are scattered across Tokyo and behind the veil, waiting their turn.

05 · The Clock
Six months to a year... and you set the pace.

An active possession slowly burns the soul it wears. Gin has months. There is no pause button on that clock... but every time you find him and anchor him, it slows. The game's whole search layer is, mechanically, a life-support machine for your brother.

06 · How You Play
An ordinary Tokyo, played on your phone.

You live real 2015 days through a period-accurate LINE super-app phone... chats, a bank, a map of real Tokyo with trains and walking distances, a to-do quest tracker, and a news feed. Most headlines are noise; some are clues. A body found in Kita-ku, a grief-circle written up in a local paper, a substation "fault" near a thin spot... the player who reads finds Gin faster. The story moves on a loop: a scene, a choice (or free text), a roll if it's uncertain, and a GM that narrates the result on rails toward a real ending.

07 · The Choice That Defines Everything
Exorcise, or absorb.

Every spirit you corner is a fork. Exorcise it and it talks... a tip, a name, the hero's road stays open. Absorb it and you take its power into yourself... and start down the villain's road, where thirteen high-grade demons wait to be bound, the worst forms of your own emotions become fuel, and you can become the next Kishin. Your powers grow by using them; the world thins around you as you do; and the people in your life shift on a relationship scale from mortal enemy to partner... with romance that can bloom even from the hostile side.

08 · Tone & Influences
Disco Elysium's head, Sir Brante's weight, World of Darkness's bite.

Reflective, literary, morally grey... your stats are habits, not numbers you grind. The occult is real esoterica, not décor: the veil is the Kabbalist Sitra Achra... literally "the Other Side," the title itself... and the villain is building the Qliphoth, the Tree of Death, the thirteen emotions in their worst form. Influences worn openly: Disco Elysium, The Life and Suffering of Sir Brante, Vampire: the Masquerade, inFAMOUS, Choice of Games.

09 · The Product
One world, two ways in.

The Book... a ~1,000-page illustrated sourcebook, written in-world as Gin's case archive: lore, cosmology, the CVD, the cults, sixty-eight character files. A complete tabletop game and a piece of fiction in its own right.

The Game... a single-file interactive edition of that same world. Choice-of-Games structure, an AI game-master on authored rails, the LINE phone, real Tokyo, living relationships, and many endings. You read the archive; then you live it.

10 · The Promise
No single ending... only the one you earn.

Save Gin, or fail him. Send Yushiro back, or take his power and fall. Stop Kishin, or become him. Tear the veil open, or let it be sealed and lose everyone on the wrong side of the wall. You are a dormant nobody with a missing brother and a clock you can't stop... only outrun. How far you'll go to keep him is the whole game.

Story Bible... the canon, locked

Every decision we've made about the deep story, in one place so nothing is lost. This is the source of truth; the book gets updated to match it.

Writing Voice... the most important thing
Between Lord of the Mysteries and Evelyn Hardcastle.

Rich description above all. Things... rooms, objects, faces, weather, the texture of the veil... are rendered in dense, sensory, specific prose. The reader should be able to see, smell, and feel every scene.

From LOTM: occult dread, ritual precision, the slow uncovering of a vast hidden system, mystery that compounds. From Evelyn Hardcastle: literary control, atmosphere, character interiority, an elegant tightening structure. The seam between them... ornate but disciplined, eerie but exact... is the house voice for the book and the game.

The Cast
Four people, two of them not people anymore.

Rei Uedera... Gin's brother, not CVD, not a partner. A criminal-investigation intern with dormant, suppressed Uedera sensitivity. His essence is Duty. His nearness is what anchors Gin. Player character of the tutorial campaign.

Gin Uedera... CVD operative, taken from the inside. Vessel of Yushiro. His doom is not dissolution: Yushiro ends him by overuse... burning power through him and suppressing him constantly until Gin forgets who he is.

Yushiro... the demon wearing Gin. One of the Thirteen: the Duty seat. A long-dead healer who crossed through Duty and refused to leave his work undone. Not evil... he wants the veil open, believing it best for everyone. To stop Kishin he will kill every cult follower with their human souls still inside; his duty is to end Kishin's work, not to save them.

Kishin... the true villain. One of the Thirteen: the Despair seat. His vessel (Fukai Rinka) is empty... the soul already erased. He doesn't murder; he turns his grieving followers into human vessels for lesser demons and evil spirits, weaponising them slowly so the CVD never notices. He reaches for the inverted crown... Thaumiel.

The Two Thirteens
Tree of Life vs Tree of Death... thirteen against thirteen.

The 13 demons (the Qliphoth seats... one per emotion, in its worst form). Goal: to possess the 13 good ones. Yushiro = Duty (in Gin); Kishin = Despair (the evil one); the other eleven are around Tokyo and behind the veil, expressed through living holders or still waiting.

The 13 of the Axis (the light anchors... Tree of Life). Goal: to kill the 13 demons. Rei, true to Duty, can become the light Duty anchor among them.

Rei's Fork... why evil erases him
Keep his Duty, or reach for something that isn't his.

The light road: stay true to Duty → become the Duty anchor → he stays himself.

The dark road: the dark Duty seat is already taken (Yushiro, in Gin), so a fallen Rei must accept a demon of another emotion. A Duty-soul can't hold a foreign emotion... it consumes him until his soul disappears. The terrible trade: if Rei becomes that vessel, Yushiro spares Gin... but both brothers stand on the dark side.

The Axis... awake, bonded, light
Not sleeping. Holding the wall, remembering he was human.

The Axis is awake but bonded to his mission, deep inside the veil as something close to pure light. The veil thins from the high-level battles; he answers by rebuilding it from his own strength, not watching the world beyond. His "waking" is not rising from sleep... it's remembering he was once a human being.

Reaching him (up to level 5): four ways carry you to him so he'll talk ...

cross
Cross the veil on an honorable mission.
die
Die.
madness
Burn power till madness.
seek
Walk the far side, faithfully searching for him.

Past level 5 in more than half your abilities: he notices you... gives more than one question, and appears in your Profile as the Axis bar (not a contact, not a relationship... a measure of closeness to god). Very rarely this opens his quest: help him remember who he is. You know nothing about him; your guesses must be exactly right to trigger a memory. Wrong, and the door stays shut.

Crossing the veil with low skills
One answer from a near-god... and you come back leaking.

Cross underpowered and you get one question answered... any question that doesn't disturb the veil balance... then you're shoved back. On return, every path you don't control flings open, uncontrolled, for a dice-roll of days. The Axis's lingering essence triggers chaos around you:

port
Random teleporting.
break
Breaking things, or hurting people.
mind
Reading minds you shouldn't... bad feelings, Madness rising.
give
Pouring your own health into the unwell around you, without control... in a train station, the loss is massive.
burn
Setting things alight.
lost
Losing yourself in the story of a place.
The Finale & The Endings
The Axis wakes, the veil falls, the world becomes a battlefield.

The endgame: the two Thirteens collide... demons trying to possess the anchors, anchors trying to kill the demons. The Axis wakes, the veil releases, the balance ends, and the battlefield spreads outward from Tokyo to the rest of the world.

The good ending: stay true to Duty → become the light Duty anchor · earn the Axis as an ally (mostly light, until he remembers his humanity) · and save Gin... from the Grid, the CVD, wherever he ends up.

The dark endings: Rei falls and is consumed by a foreign emotion (Gin spared, both lost); Yushiro opens the veil over everyone; Kishin crowns himself; or the brothers fall together.

Replayability... beyond the tutorial
Rei is the tutorial. Then the world opens.

Rei + saving Gin is the tutorial campaign... fixed and authored.

After it, the player chooses a character: 12 premades (male and female of each of the 6 paths), or a brand-new character who enters through one of the 8 start entry points... its own lore-safe story, nothing to do with Rei's campaign.

Side quests vs Romance
Two separate tracks.

Side quest = the one important unsolved thing in a person's life, a sequence of tasks in one theme (rescue them from themself · help them find/see something · pull them out of a humiliating place · knock them back to their senses). No romance in it.

Romance = a separate track, unlocked only after their side quest is solved + a threshold + courtship (gift, a meaningful place, talking about what they love, sharing a hobby). Toxic is a state, not a person: if you've done everything but the relationship slid down and you press romance anyway, it curdles... possessive, depressive, aggressive, controlling. Anyone can become a toxic romance. (Full detail in the Romance tab.)

Romance... a separate track

Romance is not a side quest. It opens only after you've done the work of knowing someone... and what it becomes depends on where the relationship actually stands.

Now authored: all 26 romanceable characters carry their route in their sheet (romance: opens, beats), gated behind their side quest + a closeness threshold + courtship, with the toxic-curdle state if you press a relationship that has slid. Hand-written explicit scenes go in the gated romance.intimate field; the AI keeps to fade-to-black on its own. Minors are never romanceable.

How romance unlocks
Earn it... it doesn't just appear.

The romance option becomes available for a character once you've solved their side quest, passed a relationship/XP threshold, and done the work of courtship:

gift
Given them a gift they'd actually value.
place
Met them somewhere that means something.
talk
Talked with them about something they love.
hobby
Done one of their hobbies with them.

Each character's secret "how to win them" stays hidden on their book sheet until you've met and learned them. Some routes open only from the hostile side (romance with someone who is, or was, an enemy).

Toxic romance... it can happen with anyone
Toxic is a state, not a person.

No one is "the toxic one." Toxicity is about where the relationship sits when you press romance. If you've done everything for someone... solved their quest, the gifts, the places... but the relationship has somehow still slid down, and you click romance anyway, it doesn't bloom. It curdles:

possessive · psychopathic · depressive · aggressive · codependent · controlling... the exact flavour depends on the person and how far down you are. A healthy romance and a toxic one can be the same character; the difference is the road you walked to get there.
