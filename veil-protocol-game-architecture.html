Playable Edition — Architecture

Working spec · v0.1 · what we've decided and what's still open

A Choice of Games–style interactive book: prose you read, choices that steer, stats tracked underneath, multiple endings — wrapped in the oil-noir illustrations and period-accurate 2015 surfaces. More book than game. AI sits only behind free-text conversation.

1 · Pillars (decided)

  • Format — long-form prose with inline choices, hidden stat tracking, and several endings. The reading is the experience. ✓
  • Engine — hybrid: an authored story spine and authored endings, with an AI layer that plays characters only during free-text conversation. ✓
  • Input — both, by context: menu choices for story beats and rolls; free-text when you're talking to a character. ✓
  • Illustration — your painterly scene stills appear at beats; a character's face is revealed the first time you go to their place. ✓
  • Surfaces — reading view + scene still + character avatar + phone / SMS + 2015 computer (email + CVD archive). ✓
  • Role & secrecy — you play the presented Rei. True sheets stay sealed; what he is, is the reveal. GM-only material never surfaces in play. ✓

4 · The multiple ends

Endings are not one final fork — they're keyed to a few tracked axes accumulated across play, checked at gates near the close:

AxisQuestion it tracks
CalmDid Rei's calm hold under pressure, or crack? (the core of the character)
The threadDid he file cases clean, or follow what doesn't fit?
NoiseHow much attention did he draw — what now knows he exists?
BondsShimizu, Okada, and the people he let close.
The truthHow much he learned about himself — and about Gin.

A small matrix (≈4–6 endings) reads these axes. Same spine, different exits — that's the "multiple ends."

5 · Relationship to the sourcebook

The 27 MB book stays the reference / GM tome. The Playable Edition is a separate app/file that draws on the same lore and the same art. A ▶ Play button in the book launches it. Keeping them separate protects the book and lets the game iterate freely.

6 · Build path (phased)

v1
Engine shell — reading view, choices, dice, stats, sealed sheet, one outline scene. (prototype started: veil-protocol-play-v1.html)
v2

v3
Diegetic surfaces — phone/SMS and the 2015 computer (email + CVD archive documents).
v4
AI conversation layer — characters answer free-text in-character, sandboxed to canon, returning to the spine.
v5
Content — full Act 1 authored, first set of endings wired to the axes.

7 · Still open (smaller, can defer)

  • AI runtime — where the conversation AI runs (API key / which model). Affects whether it works fully offline. Until then, characters use authored fallback lines.
  • Save system — checkpoint per scene vs. continuous; one playthrough vs. many.
  • Reading layout — one long scroll per scene vs. paged "tap to continue".
  • Length target — how long is the tutorial (one session? one act?).
