# Veil Protocol — Playable Side Quests (GM Book, Block 3)

*Each NPC's one important unsolved thing, drawn from their field profile. Theme tag · hook · stages · payoff. Shop/civilian contacts are errand/contact-tier by design; story NPCs run deep.*


## Spine

### Uedera Gin — The handwriting after the 14th
*The Archive is legible and trustworthy up to one date. After it, Gin is co-writing with something else.*
- ◻ Read the Archive in the order Gin demanded — out of order, it lies by omission.
- ◻ Reach Gin in a lucid window; they are shrinking.
- ◻ Separate Gin’s real intentions from Yushiro’s borrowed ones — they have started to rhyme.

**Payoff —** Whether Gin can be recovered, surrendered, or only mourned is set by how early Rei learns to tell the two voices apart.

### Uedera Rei — Find Gin — and decide what ‘found’ means
*Gin has been silent since Wednesday night. The CVD has not flagged him missing, which means someone wants it quiet.*
- ◻ Activate the Archive: four primary Grid nodes, locked behind contacts Rei has never met.
- ◻ Trace Gin’s last week — the briefing he skipped, the case he closed too fast, the address not in any file.
- ◻ Learn the shape in Gin before naming it: a top-grade of Duty does not look like possession from the outside.
- ◻ Choose the cost — every method that reaches Gin also raises Rei’s real rank, and rank is a beacon.

**Payoff —** The five roads all begin here. Gin is the reason Rei moves; what Rei becomes on the way decides which ending the reason earns.


## Antagonist

### Yushiro — Close the vacant seat
*Yushiro is not hiding. It is recruiting — through Gin’s reputation, one reasonable favour at a time.*
- ◻ Map who Gin has ‘helped’ since the 14th; each is a thread Yushiro is holding.
- ◻ Find the rite that fixes a crosser into a vacant register — and what it costs the host.
- ◻ Decide whether a fixed Yushiro is a bulwark against the cult demon or a worse throne.

**Payoff —** The two-demon structure means defeating Yushiro can hand the board to something far hungrier. Outcomes range from a clean exorcism to a devil’s alliance.


## Mirror

### Fujimoto Kenji / Abe Takeshi — Who is still home
*Fujimoto's cohabitation ended the way Gin's might — the vessel hollowed, the demon all that's left.*
- ◻ Speak with Abe and hear, without flinching, how a possession truly ends.
- ◻ Compare the Pair (Usami/Ren) and this case to read Gin's possible futures.
- ◻ Carry the warning back to how Rei chooses to reach Gin.

**Payoff —** The campaign's clearest, coldest data on what is happening to Gin — and the worst-case the player is racing to avoid.


## Ally

*Yuto is drowning in funded comfort — and Rei might be the foothold that gets him to ask for help, once, sober.*
- ◻ Like him for himself, not the name or the easy rounds.
- ◻ Be steady through the relapses without becoming his enabler or his savior.
- ◻ At depth he reaches his family and asks, sober, for help — a rescue or a tragedy.

**Payoff —** A wealthy, well-connected ally pulled back from the edge — or the grief of holding the after when the ask never comes.
