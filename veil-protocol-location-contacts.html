Location Contacts — The People of the Places
One or two encounterable people for each place in the gazetteer. This batch covers the Gatherings venues. Full-depth sheets with the new Reputation block: standing by sphere, what people know (and don’t), how wide their net runs, and who they’re tied to. + green = warm tie, – red = friction, red = GM secret. Portrait boxes are image slots to fill.

Mergeable into the book’s character chapter on your say-so. The Reputation block here is the template I’ll backfill onto the 25 existing sheets.

Gatherings · the venue contacts

☀npc_kaneda
Kaneda Tōru 金田 徹
Bartender · The Lighthouse, Ni-chōme
Civilian (aware)WitnessNi-chōme fixtureUnbribable
Identity
54. Has tended the same eight stools for twenty years. Not a practitioner, but he has served enough of them to know exactly what he is looking at and to never once say so.
Lives
Above the bar, a small flat up a second stair; he has not lived more than fifty metres from that counter since 1994.
Works
The Lighthouse, every night but Monday. Opens at eight, closes when the last regular is safe to send home.
Habits
Polishes glasses he has already polished while he decides about you. Walks the lost ones to the station himself. Never drinks on the clock.
Capability
Rank noneAbility noneHuman skills Read-people 9 · Memory-for-faces 10 · Discretion 10
Reputation
In the Grid
Trusted as a safe pair of ears. Grid people send the frightened to him.
In the CVD
Effectively unknown; a name in no file, which is how he has stayed useful.
In Ni-chōme
An institution. Two streets that would close ranks around him without being asked.
Known for
Remembering everyone, repeating almost no one.
Not known
How much he has actually pieced together about the veil over twenty years. More than he lets on.
Network
Deep but narrow — everyone who matters in nightlife Shinjuku; thin past the ward line.
Ties
Choi Hana (a regular he looks out for) · Gin (mutual quiet respect; Gin buys the room a round) · Majima (served him once, never again — “he sells the people who drink here”)
Appearance · Personality
Grey, neat, forearms like a man who has lifted kegs for thirty years. Warm, watchful, immovable. Kindness as a discipline, not a mood.
Ask for nothing and tip well and he will, eventually, tell you the one true thing you came for. Push and the shutter comes down for good. — G.U.
☀npc_endo
Endō Masa 遠藤 政
Doorman · Vault, Roppongi
Sensitive (low)Door · SecuritySees everyone
Identity
38. Built like a wall, voice like a lullaby. A faint sensitivity he never trained — enough to feel a wrong one coming up the stairs before he sees them.
Lives
Azabu, a sublet over a laundry; sleeps days, works the door of two clubs by night.
Works
Vault, Thursday to Sunday. Knows who came in afraid of whom.
Habits
Memorises the room from the door. Walks women to taxis off the clock. Lifts on his nights off, alone.
Capability
Rank Sensitive (untrained)Ability faint danger-senseHuman skills Intimidation 8 · Crowd-read 9 · Calm-under-threat 9
Reputation
In the Grid
A known safe door. If Endo lets you in, you can lose a tail inside.
In the CVD
Flagged once as a low sensitive, never followed up; he keeps it that way.
In Roppongi
Respected by every door on the strip. Owes and is owed favours up and down it.
Known for
Never forgetting a face and never starting a fight he can end with a sentence.
Not known
That he can feel the bad ones; he reads it as “a gut thing” and is more often right than any scanner.
Network
Wide in nightlife security, shallow elsewhere; a phone full of bouncers and cab drivers.
Ties
Shimizu Ren (a regular he quietly watches over) · Kuroda Sei (lets him hide inside on bad nights) · Gin (Gin uses the mezzanine fire door; Endo pretends not to notice)
Appearance · Personality
Huge, shaven-headed, soft-spoken. Gentle by default, terrifying on the one occasion a year it is needed. Protective of the young and the lost.
Tell him who you are avoiding, never who you are meeting. He keeps the first kind of secret for free. — G.U.
☀npc_hara_setsu
Hara Setsu 原 節
Bath-house keeper · Tsuru-no-yu, Kita-ku
Civilian (knowing)Neighbourhood memoryReads the dead
Identity
71. Has run the high desk at Tsuru-no-yu for forty years. Knows the ward’s dead — who they were, where they lingered — better than the ward office knows its living.
Lives
The rooms behind the bath house; the chimney is her landmark and her livelihood.
Works
Tsuru-no-yu, every day it is open, which is every day but the second Tuesday.
Habits
Leaves a dish of salt at the door. Talks to regulars who died years ago, calmly, and is usually right that they are listening.
Capability
Rank Sensitive (folk)Ability faint clairsentience, untrainedHuman skills Local-history 10 · Herbs & cleansing 7 · Patience 10
Reputation
In the Grid
Quietly revered by the old guard. Tanizaki’s generation; younger Grid barely know her name.
In the CVD
Off every record. She predates the institution’s interest and outlasts it.
In Kita-ku
A landmark in her own right. Three generations of the neighbourhood have soaked under her eye.
Known for
Knowing which corner of which street something happened on, and when.
Not known
That her “chats with the regulars” are functioning grief-readings she has done untrained for fifty years.
Network
Narrow and old — the neighbourhood and its dead. Worth more than a hundred live contacts for a Kita case.
Ties
Tanizaki Seiichi (old friends; they do not need to speak) · Katagiri Noa (comes to thaw out; Setsu spoils her) · Tendo Akira (the only one who comes before dawn; she lets him be) · Gin & Rei (she has told Gin twice to bring his brother more often)
Appearance · Personality
Tiny, straight-backed, sharp-eyed. Unhurried. Will talk for an hour if you are clean and patient, and not at all if you are in a rush.
If you want the true history of a Kita thin spot, you do not file a request. You take a long bath and you listen to Mrs. Hara. — G.U.
☀npc_mariko
“Mariko” 真理子 — working name
Worker · The Peony Rooms, near Uguisudani
CivilianWatches the desperateProtectivereal name withheld
Identity
40s. Has worked back rooms like this one longer than she will say, by choices that were never really choices. No ability — but a hard-won eye for who is hiding and who is hunting.
Lives
A weekly room nearby; she keeps her real name and her real address apart from this place on purpose.
Works
The Peony Rooms, nights mostly. Has watched a dozen lost people pass through and quietly helped a few out the back.
Habits
Keeps a kettle going for the other workers. Clocks every new face. Never talks where the desk can hear.
Capability
Rank noneAbility noneHuman skills Street-sense 9 · Reading-desperation 10 · Keeping-quiet 10
Reputation
In the Grid
A whispered name among those who do outreach to the fallen; the rest of the Grid does not know she exists.
In the CVD
Invisible, and safer for it.
On the strip
Trusted by the other workers, mistrusted by the men who run it — the correct ratio.
Known for
Nothing, by design. That is the whole skill.
Not known
She has quietly gotten at least one lost medium out of the building before the cells or the Grid found them.
Network
Tiny and precious — the workers, one outreach nurse, and now, perhaps, you.
Ties
Choi Hana (the one Grid contact she half-trusts) · the management (mutual, careful contempt) · Gin (helped her once; she has never forgotten which kind of operative he was)
Appearance · Personality
Tired, careful, kinder than her circumstances should allow. Talks to you away from the desk or not at all, and never in a way that costs her.
Treat her as a person, because she is one, and because she remembers exactly who did not. If you find one of ours here, you get them out first and write it up second. — G.U.

Gotō Hideki 後藤 秀樹
Floor manager · Tamaya Hall, Kabukichō
CivilianLaundererSells silence
Identity
47. Runs the floor from the glass booth. Knows precisely what the back rooms are for and takes a clean cut of all of it.
Lives
A serviced high-rise in Nishi-Shinjuku, paid in cash, nothing in his name.
Works
Tamaya Hall, the booth, most hours it is open — which is most hours.
Habits
Counts the floor by ear. Trades the quiet back rooms for a favour or a flat fee. Owes someone above him and never says who.
Capability
Rank noneAbility noneHuman skills Money 9 · Negotiation 8 · Self-preservation 10
Reputation
In the Grid
A necessary evil. Useful, never trusted.
In the CVD
A name in a financial-crimes file nobody has the budget to action.
In Kabukichō
Connected upward, feared sideways. The men above him are the real answer to any question.
Known for
Renting silence by the hour. The most soundproof rooms in the ward.
Not known
Who he answers to. Pull that thread and the campaign gets a great deal larger.
Network
Wide and dirty — money, muscle, and the people who move both.
Ties
Majima Kenji (regular customer and occasional partner) · Endō Masa (old grudge, different strip) · the CVD (avoids; one audit from a bad year)
Appearance · Personality
Sharp suit, soft hands, a smile that arrives a half-second late. Transactional to the bone. Will not lie to you — lying costs more than the truth.
You can buy an hour of true silence here. Just remember someone sells him yours just as cheaply. — G.U.
☀npc_serizawa
Serizawa 芹沢
Head bartender · Kogane, Ginza
CivilianKeeper of the roomReads money
Identity
60s. Thirty years behind the same brass counter. Has served the Tower families and the CVD’s upper floor so long he can tell you who is rising and who is quietly finished by who sits where.
Lives
A modest, immaculate flat in Tsukishima; the contrast with his clientele does not trouble him.
Works
Kogane, six nights. Decides who belongs with a glance and is never wrong.
Habits
Carves the ice by hand. Forgets nothing he hears and repeats none of it — to anyone who cannot afford the room.
Capability
Rank noneAbility noneHuman skills Discretion 10 · Class-read 10 · Etiquette 10
Reputation
Among the Towers
A trusted fixture. They speak freely near him because he has earned, over decades, the right to be furniture.
In the CVD
Known to the directors, unknown to the operatives. Adachi has a usual.
In the Grid
Out of reach; a different city, financially.
Known for
Knowing who is meeting whom in Ginza before their offices do.
Not known
How much of the city’s real power he has mapped from behind that counter. He will not sell it. He might, once, give it.
Network
Narrow and stratospheric — money, blood, and ministry. Useless for a street case, decisive for a political one.
Ties
Tsukishiro Haruki (knows the family three generations deep) · Director Adachi (a standing usual) · Gin (Gin cannot afford the room; Serizawa is unfailingly, pointedly polite about it)
Appearance · Personality
Silver, spare, precise to the millimetre. Courtesy as armour. Warm to no one and gracious to all.
You will learn nothing from anyone in that room. You will learn everything from where Serizawa seats them. — G.U.
☀npc_beckman
“The Beck’s Man” — Ōtsuka, the platform clerk
Counter clerk · Beck’s Stand, Platform 3, Shibuya
CivilianSees nothing, on purposeMinor
Identity
50s, name-tag says Ötsuka. Has poured the same cheap coffee on the same platform for years and has perfected not noticing anything. The most useful witness in the city because he refuses to be one.
Lives
Nerima, an ordinary flat, an ordinary life; would be horrified to learn what passes across his counter.
Works
Beck’s Stand, Platform 3, mornings and evenings; four hundred strangers an hour.
Habits
Looks at the cup, not the customer. Will not remember you, which is the point.
Reputation
Everywhere
None. He is scenery — and scenery is the safest cover a meeting can have.
Known for
Speed and indifference, in that order.
Ties
None he is aware of. Gin has passed more folded notes across this counter than across any desk; Ōtsuka has never once looked up.
Not a contact — a condition. Use the stand for what it is: three accountable minutes and a man who will never describe you. — G.U.
