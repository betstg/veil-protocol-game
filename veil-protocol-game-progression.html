Three things grow three different ways. Attributes grow by living (your habits). Ability paths grow by use (do the thing). Skills are bought with XP (earned from successful work). And the stronger you get, the thinner the veil around you — so the world rises to meet you.

1The Dice — two kinds of roll CANON

A d6 pool; 4+ = success; 0 / 1 / 2 / 3+ = fail / partial / solid / strong.

  • Ability roll = Rank dice + Path level. Stability loss removes rank dice from the pool.
  • Human roll = Attribute + Skill — e.g. Body + Combat to trade punches, Charisma + Social to charm a witness.

2Track 1 — Attributes grow by living NEW

The flat human capacities. You do not buy them — they rise from how you act and what you choose. Charm people and Charisma climbs; fight and Body climbs; keep your nerve and Composure climbs. Six of them:

3Track 2 — Ability Paths grow by use CHANGED

Six paths × 10 levels. You do not spend XP on them. Using a path makes it grow — read scenes and Clairsentience climbs; nudge minds and Telepathy climbs. (The book costed levels at 100 XP; we replace that with a usage threshold per level — the more demanding the use, the more it counts.)

  • Opening a path CANON — not grindable, not bought. A recognition event the campaign triggers. Rei: Clairsentience open, a 2nd dormant. Once open, it grows by use.
  • L4 and above CANON — still gated by a narrative unlock; raw usage gets you to the door, the story opens it.
  • Path skills (the wheel's techniques) NEW — unlock as the path levels; the wheel is the UI.
  • Multi-path tension CANON — every open path beyond the first = +5 Noise, permanently. More range, more attention. This feeds the rising-world loop (§6).

4Track 3 — Skills, and the XP pool CHANGED

XP is one pool, earned from successful work, and it is spent on one thing: your learned Human Skills — Investigation, Social, Stealth, Combat, Symbol Work, Research. (The book let XP buy everything; here XP is the skills currency only. Attributes grow by living, paths by use.)

5Rank — and why power is dangerous CHANGED

The ladder CANON: Sensitive → Ritualist → Channel → Arbiter → Vanguard (→ Singularity). Rank = the rank dice in your pool, gated by path levels + narrative events.

The rising world

You did not want a taper — you want the opposite, and it's better. The veil is thinner around stronger mediums. So as Rei rises:

  • bigger demons and spirits are drawn to him — he shines through the veil and the hungry things notice;
  • the quests get harder and pay more XP;
  • and the numbers climb with him — the thresholds, the DCs, the bars all scale up.

Growing strong is not getting safe. It is becoming a brighter target in a darker room.

6The Veil-Balance loop — the bars + CANON

Re-explaining the one you flagged. The "veil meter" is simply: how thin is the veil around Rei right now. It is not a map of districts — it's him. It rises with his rank, his open paths (+5 Noise each), and his recent power use; it falls when he lies low and stays ordinary.

The loop: use power → solve the scene, but raise Noise (→ Architect attention / Correction CANON) and thin the veil around you → thinner veil draws more and stronger entities to you → harder fights, bigger rewards, more chaos. Power buys the moment and bills the next one. The skill is knowing when to spend and when to go be a normal nineteen-year-old for a day.

The three bars: Stability (short — each use spends it; empty pulls rank dice) CANON · Madness (long — lowered only by normalcy) CANON · Veil-thinness around you (the spawn driver above) NEW.

9The numbers, updated CHANGED

  • Attributes & Human Skills cap at 20, and both grow by correct use — a hidden % that fills after a quest, battle, or mission where you used them well, then ticks the number up. (This extends "skills bought with XP" → skills also grow by doing; the XP pool's remaining role is flagged below.)
  • Three vitals, all 0–100: Stability, Madness, and Veil-thinness-around-you. You actively manage all three.
  • Profile bars are tap/hover-explained — every bar tells you what it does.

10Exorcise vs Absorb — the two faces of power NEW

When you beat a spirit or demon, you choose: exorcise it (cross / clear it) or absorb it (take its power).

  • Exorcise — the merciful road. A cooperative spirit or demon, spared, may give you tips — even leads about Gin. Information is the reward.
  • Absorb — the hungry road. You take the power and feed the thing inside you. This is the villain path.

11The Possession Path — the villain arc NEW

  • You can choose to be possessed. The full dark build: take thirteen high-rank demons as contracted possessors — "acceptable" demons with complex personalities (like Ren in Usami Hinata), each a signed contract.
  • Each demon feeds on one emotion. To use its power you must use / feed that emotion — which pulls you toward it and away from your Duty (rescuing Ren, holding your domain).
  • The emotions now live in the player. Rei carries the thirteen emotion-domains as a personal system. Using a demon advances its emotion. The more Duty you spend, the more you stagnate in the Duty slot (the 13th) — you become Duty itself.
  • So the endgame tension is emotional balance + Madness control: lean too hard on one demon's emotion, or let Madness fill, and you are lost — a villain wearing thirteen contracts.

12Grounding Object — rank by meaning NEW

A grounding object steadies you (resist possession & Madness). Its rank is set by what it means to you, not what it is — a mother's photograph outranks a cactus… unless you never had a mother and the cactus is the thing that once saved your life. Meaning, weighed honestly, sets the number.

13NPCs, sidequests & relationships NEW

  • Almost every talkable NPC has a sidequest tied to them — and it takes you to places (their corner of the city, their people).
  • Relationships run −100 to +100, rendered angry 😠 ↔ ❤ heart, neutral at zero. They move at a tabletop pace — a few points (+3, +1, +5) per meaningful interaction, not per click.
  • A person's bio is what they wrote — first person, in their own voice, as vague or guarded as they are.
